## Post-hoc diagnostics (not pre-registered)

Added after the results above, to understand them. They do not change any verdict.

- **The LSTM has a short memory and nearly ignores commands.** Only 13% of the injected value spikes and 4% of the injected commands moved the forecast by ≥ 0.05. When a spike does move it, IG ranks its lag in the top 3 in 65% of cases [46%, 81%], n = 26. Narratives that credit the channel's own recent values therefore describe the model faithfully: that is what it uses.
- **The deletion test was mis-specified.** Ranking by \|IG\| also removes inputs that lower the error, so resetting them raised it (error reduced in 36% of windows). Ranking by signed IG reduces the error in 64% of windows, against 45% for random inputs. Zero-baseline deletion is also out of distribution (Hooker et al. 2019), so the means above are dominated by a few windows.
- **Band coverage fails on shifted channels.** Median per-channel coverage is 87%; the mean is pulled down by a few channels whose test period differs from validation (the shift found in ADR 0006; A-5 was already flagged in the Phase 1 data card).
