## Protocol

- **Thresholds never see labels.** Each detector's threshold is the 0.999 quantile of its scores on the nominal validation tail (`val_quantile`). Test labels are used only to score the predictions.
- **Composite F1 is the headline** (Garg et al. 2021): point-wise precision combined with event-wise recall. It rewards finding every anomaly and penalises flooding operators with alarm steps.
- **Event F1** follows the dataset's own paper (Hundman et al. 2018). Micro averaging sums counts over channels; macro averaging takes the mean of per-channel F1 and is robust to one noisy channel.
- ⚠ **Point-adjusted F1 is inflated** and shown only to compare with papers. Random scores score highly on it (Kim et al., AAAI 2022).
- † **Oracle** picks the best of many thresholds per channel *using* test labels. It is never an operating point, and it is a **loose** bound: selecting per channel overfits to channels with only one or two events (random scores reach 0.74).
- Delays and false-alarm rates are in **time steps**: NASA did not publish the sampling interval, so we do not convert them to minutes or days.
- 95% CIs come from 1,000 bootstrap resamples of channels.
